# Raw Inputs

Dump unprocessed content here. Call transcripts, data exports, articles, voice memos, screenshots, PDFs.

## What is worth saving

- **Call notes and transcripts.** Keep the buyer's exact words. They are what your first lines and objection handling get built from.
- **Closed-won and closed-lost exports from your CRM.** The `icp-modeller` skill builds your ICP from who actually bought, not from who you hoped would.

CRM exports contain personal data. Keep them out of a repo you share more widely than the people allowed to see them.

## How to process

Tell Claude:

```
Read everything in raw/ and organize it into wiki/ pages.
Group by topic and summarize the key insights.
```

Files stay here as the original source. Structured output goes into wiki/.
